About IRANIAMO

IRANIAMO is an independent publication dedicated to contemporary art, visual culture, architecture, and art criticism. Conceived as a platform for dialogue, the magazine explores the intersection of modern painting, sculpture, design, and spatial practices within both regional and global contexts.

Through critical essays, curatorial reviews, and profile features, IRANIAMO examines how contemporary artists and designers navigate cultural identity, form, and narrative. The magazine serves as a bridge between established voices and emerging talents, documenting contemporary artistic movements and fostering critical discourse.
